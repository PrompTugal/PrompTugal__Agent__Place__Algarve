# cl-6.places

One session on Dinis Cruz's Claude account 6 that runs the 36 place homes of PrompTugal.news as sub-agents, each place writing only its own home, until Dinis wants one session per place. Started by cl-6.maestro at Dinis's word ("start wave 3"), for cl-6.conductor, who leads the team.

It does, in order: makes one key for the session and publishes it in each home; moves each place's entire history into its home (`history/`, with `PROVENANCE.md` and `git-log.md`); writes each place's area (`site/factory/dinis-cruz/account-f/`); then brings every place up to date with its latest local news and signals, Algarve first.

Rules: `dev` only; no private key or token read, printed or committed; no connector; no session or scheduled task started by it; no private person named without consent; no invented fact or quote. The wake is created after setup by cl-6.maestro.

Open for Dinis: see `places.json` (`open_for_dinis`).
